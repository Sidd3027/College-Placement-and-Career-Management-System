Frequently asked

How many jobs can I apply to?

You can apply to every job you are eligible for. Check the eligibility on each job page first.

Can I change my resume after applying?

Update your resume in My Profile. New applications will use the updated file.

What if I have an active backlog?

Many companies do not accept active backlogs. The job page shows the rule for each company.
