About the portal

College placement drives involve continuous coordination between students, recruiters, and placement officers. When job notifications, eligibility rules, applications, and interview schedules are scattered across spreadsheets and emails, opportunities are missed.

This portal unifies the entire campus recruitment lifecycle into one structured workspace—giving each stakeholder dedicated tools and real-time visibility.

For students

  • Academic profile & resume upload
  • Automatic CGPA & branch eligibility check
  • Live application status tracking
  • Interview schedules & career resources

For companies

  • Dedicated company profile
  • Post & manage campus job openings
  • Review & shortlist applicants
  • Schedule technical & HR rounds

For administrators

  • Approve companies & job drives
  • Manage student & application records
  • Record final placement offers
  • Branch-wise analytics & CSV reports
